ScanRelay 0.2.5 lets you assign specific people to a printer.

## What changed

- A printer's form has "People assigned to this printer". Type a name, choose the person, save. They are listed on that printer on top of its group. Only people ScanRelay has already synced can be chosen: someone new must first be in a sync group, or be added on the People page, and a sync must have run.
- A printer can be set to "Only the people assigned below". It then lists just those people: no group is used, not even the default one, and people added by hand to every printer are left off. This is for a printer that belongs to one person or a few.
- An assigned person who is no longer synced, because they left every sync group or were excluded, comes off the printer at the next sync like anyone else.
- Changes take effect at the next sync. Switching a printer from a large group to a few assigned people removes many entries at once, so the change guard may hold that sync for an administrator to approve on the Runs page.
- The modules are unchanged: Kyocera ECOSYS 0.2.0 and Canon imageCLASS 0.2.0.

## Steps for this version

The normal update. Run the installer on the server as an administrator. The database is updated by itself when the service starts.
